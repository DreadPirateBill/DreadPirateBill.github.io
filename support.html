Which devices does Sprocket Player run on?

Apple TV running tvOS 18.6 or later, and iPad running iPadOS 17.6 or later. It is not available for iPhone.

Does Sprocket Player need Plex, Jellyfin or Kodi installed?

No. It reads media straight from an SMB or NFS share. If you already use one of those servers, point Sprocket Player at the same folders and it will pick up your artwork and metadata.

Why do I get an “SMB2 or higher required” error from a Windows PC?

Sprocket Player uses SMB2 and above. Windows 10 and later have SMB1 disabled, but some older shares still advertise only SMB1. Enable SMB2 in Windows Features and try again.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on the device. Credentials live in the system Keychain and thumbnails are encrypted on disk. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
