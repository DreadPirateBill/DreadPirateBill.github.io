Intro detection

Skip the intro. Every episode.

Turn on Intro Detection and Sprocket Player listens to a season in the background, fingerprints the title sequence, and finds it in every episode. When you hit it, a Skip Intro button is waiting.

  • Works on your own files, with no online database and no server plugin.
  • Analysis runs entirely on your Apple TV or iPad, while you browse.
  • New episodes are picked up automatically the next time you visit the season.
  • Off by default. Enable it in Options when you're ready.

On device. No transcoding.

The file you have is the file you watch.

Media servers re-encode video on the fly when a client can't handle it, burning CPU and softening the picture. Sprocket Player decodes on the device itself, so every stream is the original bitstream at full quality, straight off your NAS.

  • No server-side transcoder, no hardware-acceleration licence, no waiting for a session to spin up.
  • Your NAS only has to serve files. A basic share on any box is enough.
  • Switch audio tracks and subtitles instantly, with no re-encode.
  • Thumbnails are generated by the device too, and cached for next time.

Privacy first

Your library is nobody's business.

Sprocket Player has no backend, no account, and no cloud. It talks to your shares and nothing else. Privacy isn't a setting we added later. It's how the app is built.

Local only

No server component and no account. Your media and browsing history never leave your network.

Keychain credentials

Share usernames and passwords are stored in the system Keychain on the device, and nowhere else.

PIN-secured sources

Lock a source behind a PIN. Its thumbnails and history are encrypted at rest with AES-256, keyed from that PIN.

Auto-disconnect

Choose to drop share connections when you leave the app or go idle, so a shared living-room TV stays a shared TV.

The only outbound connection is anonymous crash reporting, which can be turned off in Options. Read the full privacy policy.
