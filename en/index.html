Bring your own library

The perfect companion to Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and Kodi libraries.

Your library, anywhere you want it to be. Whether you run a popular media server or prefer a network file share, Sprocket lets you enjoy your library with no compromises.

  • Sign in to Plex, Jellyfin or Emby and your movies and shows are there, with the posters, descriptions and episode details you already know.
  • No media server? Point Sprocket at a shared folder on your NAS or computer. Posters and descriptions saved alongside your files by Kodi and similar library tools show up automatically.
  • Videos play in their original quality. If you use a media server, it can step in and convert a file that otherwise wouldn't play, and Sprocket tells you when that happens.
  • Pick up where you left off. Sprocket remembers what you've watched and can hide it with one toggle.
  • Keep a film series together. On a shared folder, Collections gather every movie in a franchise into one place.

Intro detection

Skip the intro. Every episode.

Turn on Intro Detection and Sprocket finds the opening titles in your shows for you. When they start, a Skip Intro button appears. One press and you're into the episode.

  • Works with the shows you already have. Nothing to download, and nothing to install on your server.
  • Private by design. It all happens on your Apple TV or iPad.
  • New episodes are covered automatically as you add them.
  • Off until you want it. Switch it on in Options.

Play it your way

The file you have is the file you watch.

Sprocket plays your videos in their original quality, from wherever they live. Use what you already have and it just works. And when a video needs a hand, a media server can convert it for you.

  • Simplest: use what you have. A media server or a shared folder, Sprocket is at home with either.
  • Fastest: a shared folder with your library already organised. Nothing sits between Sprocket and your files, so browsing and playback are at their quickest.
  • Older Apple TV, or watching away from home? A media server can convert video to suit the device and the connection.
  • Two video engines, VLC and MPV. Choose the one that looks best on your setup, with HDR on Apple TV 4K.

Privacy first

Your library is nobody's business.

No account to create. No tracking. Nobody looking over your shoulder. Sprocket connects to your media, and that is where it stops.

Your library, your business

There is no Sprocket account to sign up for and no Sprocket cloud. Your library goes from your home to your screen, with nobody in between.

Nothing looked up online

Posters and details come from your own server or your own files. Sprocket doesn't go out to the internet to work out what's in your collection.

Encrypted, always

Thumbnails and details Sprocket keeps on your device are always encrypted. Add a PIN to anything you'd rather keep to yourself and it's locked tighter still.

Walks away when you do

Sprocket can disconnect when you leave the app or go idle, so a shared living-room TV stays a shared TV.

Sprocket only talks to the places you point it at. If you use Plex, that includes your Plex account, which signs you in and helps Sprocket find your server. Cast photos are the one exception: they load from the web addresses saved in your library's files. Passwords and sign-ins are kept in your device's secure Keychain. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on. Read the full privacy policy.

Apple TV and iPad

One app, designed for the Siri Remote at ten feet and for touch on the couch. Same library either way.

Wallpapers

Bundled photo and animated backgrounds, or long-press any photo in your library and make it the wallpaper.

Sort your way

Name, date, size, recently viewed or random. Favourites float to the top, and each folder remembers how you sorted it.

Collections

Group every film in a franchise on a file share into one folder, even the later ones that stopped following the naming pattern.

Picture controls

Aspect, deinterlace, speed, brightness and colour, plus upscaling, debanding and tone mapping on the MPV engine. Adjust live while the picture plays, per file if you like.

Set up from your phone

Scan the QR code on your Apple TV and fill in the address, name and PIN on your phone's keyboard. Sign in to Plex or Jellyfin with a short code, never a password on the remote.
