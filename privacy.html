Sprocket Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Sprocket Player is a media browser and player for iPad and Apple TV. This document describes what data the app handles and what happens to it.

What Sprocket Player does with your data

Local-only by design. Sprocket Player connects to file shares on your local network (SMB, NFS) and reads from your device's photo library to display and play your photos and videos. The app does not have its own backend server, does not require an account, and does not transmit your media or browsing activity to us or to any third party.

Source credentials. Usernames and passwords for SMB and NFS shares you configure are stored locally on your device in the iOS/tvOS Keychain. They never leave your device.

Preferences and thumbnails. Your settings, source lists, watched-state, and generated thumbnails are stored locally on your device. When a source is marked as secured with a PIN, the associated thumbnails and preference records are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM via Apple's CryptoKit. The encryption keys are derived from the PIN and stored in the Keychain.

Crash and performance data

Sprocket Player uses Sentry to capture crash reports and basic performance metrics. This data is sent to Sentry's servers, used only to identify and fix bugs, and is not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

The categories of data Sentry collects:

  • Crash data: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and the state of the app at the moment of a crash
  • Performance data: anonymous timing and resource-usage metrics

No personally identifying information, no media files, no source credentials, and no contents of your photo library are ever sent to Sentry or anywhere else.

Network access

Sprocket Player uses your device's local network access to discover SMB shares via Bonjour and to connect to file shares you configure. iOS will prompt you to grant local network permission the first time the app needs it. The app does not connect to any servers on the public internet other than Sentry (for crash reporting, as described above).

Children's privacy

Sprocket Player is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
